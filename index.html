I received my Ph.D. in Computer and Information Science from the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Mark Yatskar. Previously, I was a Student Researcher at Google in Augmented Reality and an AI Research Intern at Salesforce Research.

MapTrace·Preprint · 2025

Teaching models to read maps

Multimodal models can recognize a map yet struggle to follow a route. MapTrace generates synthetic maps and route annotations to train this spatial skill.

Public release: two million annotated map–path examples.

X-InstructBLIP·ECCV 2024

Connecting images, audio, video, and 3D to language models

A framework for connecting image, audio, video, and 3D inputs to a frozen language model. Independently trained modality adapters support reasoning across multiple input types.

Evaluated across 16 benchmarks; includes generated instruction data and the DisCRn benchmark.

PaperCode & models

Related work: Contra4 (EMNLP 2025). A benchmark for contrastive reasoning across up to four modalities. Project & data

ViUniT·CVPR 2025

Testing the reasoning behind an answer

Visual programs can produce the right answer with faulty logic. ViUniT generates image–answer test cases to check that logic and uses the results for program selection, repair, and learning.

Average accuracy gains of 11.4 percentage points across three datasets in the paper’s visual-programming experiments.

PaperProjectCodeCoauthored explainerMarkTechPost coverage
Interactive Visual Gym·Preprint · 2026

Evaluating assistants in interactive worlds

An assistant’s advice changes what a user does next. Interactive Visual Gym simulates users and task environments so models can be evaluated and trained through the consequences of their guidance.

Cooking, household tasks, and mobile interfaces; 315 benchmark interaction scenarios.
